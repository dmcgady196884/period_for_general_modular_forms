2. ADDITION A: bare-Mellin reduction of the wall-crossing residue

LEMMA (s-pole structure of the T-kernel).
Fix tau_p in H, k even nonzero. The kernel
  kT(tau_p,s) = zeta(1-s,tau_p+1) - e^{i pi(s-1)} zeta(s-(k-1),tau_p+1)
is meromorphic in s with exactly two simple poles, at s=0 and s=k:
  Res_{s=0} kT = -1,   Res_{s=k} kT = +1,
both tau_p-independent. The kernel tau_p^{s-1} is entire in s.
Proof: Hurwitz zeta(nu,a) has one simple pole at nu=1, residue 1
(DLMF 25.11). 1st term: pole at s=0, zeta(1-s,.)=-1/s+O(1), res -1;
regular at s=k. 2nd term: pole at s=k, zeta(s-k+1,.)=1/(s-k)+O(1),
prefactor -e^{i pi(k-1)}=+1 (k even), res +1; regular at s=0. The
exponential tau_p^{s-1} is entire.

COROLLARY (s-poles of the wall-crossing residue).
In eq:residue-formula
  R_{tau_p}(f,s) = -2 pi i * i^{-s} Res(f,tau_p)
                   [ sigS tau_p^{s-1} + sigT kT(tau_p,s) ]:
the S-part is entire in s; the T-part has simple poles only at s=0,k:
  Res_{s=0} R = +2 pi i * sigT * Res(f,tau_p),
  Res_{s=k} R = -2 pi i * i^{-k} * sigT * Res(f,tau_p).
So R is entire in s  iff  sigT = 0.

REMARK. The constant mode gives the special-value poles
-c_f(0)(1/s + i^k/(k-s)). An S-crossing shifts L* by an entire-in-s
amount and cannot disturb them; a T-crossing can. So 1806 sec 2.4's
"special values insensitive to wall-crossing" = the claim sigT=0 at
every crossed pole = the cusp-limit class of the Proposition below.

PROPOSITION (reduction to 1806 Eq.(2.65)).
Let f in F_k be regular at the cusp; two admissible pairs in the
cusp-limit class of cor:1806-coincide, differing by one crossing of an
interior pole tau_p. Then sigT=0 (the T-segment retreats to the cusp
and dies by lem:cusp-decay(a)), and
  R_{tau_p}(f,s) = -2 pi i * i^{-s} * sigS * Res(f,tau_p) * tau_p^{s-1},
entire in s, equal to the single-pole term of 1806 Eq.(2.65) under
  chi(tau_p) = -sigS,
  Res(m_i,s,tau_i) = i^{-s} Res(f,tau_i) tau_i^{s-1}
(residue of the bare-Mellin integrand i^{-s} f(tau) tau^{s-1}).

SCOPE: needs cusp regularity. By lem:cusp-decay(b) a cusp pole of
order M makes the T-segment grow like exp(2 pi M Im tau0); cannot be
dropped. Two basepoints act oppositely: tau0=i kills S (completed-L /
BFK form), tau0->i*inf kills T (bare-Mellin). HONEST WEIGHT: the
Proposition is a consistency repackaging of cor:1806-coincide + the
residue theorem; the only new content is the Lemma (s-poles) and its
special-value consequence in the Corollary.

3. ADDITION B: L-function with poles interior AND at the cusp

LEMMA (cusp-part matching by a j-polynomial).
Let k even with S_{2-k}={0} -- in particular every weight with
dim S_k = 1, i.e. k in {12,16,18,20,22,26}. Write k = 12 l + k' with
k' in {0,4,6,8,10,14}, and set
  f_k^min := E_{k'} Delta^l
the least-polar generator of M_k^! (for l<0: a pole only at the cusp,
order -l; for l>=0 use E_k = 1 + O(q) instead). Since
j = q^{-1} + 744 + O(q) is holomorphic on H with a simple cusp pole,
for any f in F_k there is a polynomial P with
  g := P(j) f_k^min  in  M_k^!
whose cusp principal part equals that of f and with no poles in H
(equivalently g is in the span of the Duke-Jenkins canonical basis
{f_k^min j^m}, P determined triangularly in the cusp Laurent data, g
unique up to M_k). Then f+g in F_k is regular at the cusp.

THEOREM (per-mode closed form, interior and cusp poles).
Let k be as above and f in F_k have poles both in H (data
r*_{f,tau_p}(m)) and at the cusp; take g as in the Lemma. Then
L*(f,s) is finite for every s at every finite tau0 (lem:Lstar-finite,
no cuspidality needed), equals L*(f+g,s) - L*(g,s) independently of
the choice of g, and has the per-mode closed form

  L*(f,s) =
     - c_f(0) ( 1/s + i^k/(k-s) )
     + sum_{n != 0} c_{Rf}(n) [ Gamma(s,2 pi n)/(2 pi n)^s
                                + i^k Gamma(k-s,2 pi n)/(2 pi n)^{k-s} ]
     + sum_{tau_p} sum_{m>0} r*_{f,tau_p}(m)
              [ I_{m-1}(s,tau_p) + i^k I_{m-1}(k-s,tau_p) ],

IDENTICAL IN FORM to eq:interior-pole-modes, where now the
coefficients c_{Rf}(n) of (1 - Phat) f include the finitely many
cusp-polar modes (n<0), each evaluated to the same incomplete-gamma
bracket by the unfolding of prop:hurwitz-unfold (entire in nu, hence
valid on polar modes).

Proof. Finiteness: lem:Lstar-finite. Linearity:
L*(f) = L*(f+g) - L*(g) on a common admissible contour (f and f+g
share interior poles; g has none). Apply eq:interior-pole-modes to f+g
(regular at cusp, same interior data since g is holomorphic on H) and
eq:bfk-eq-statement to g. Since Phat g = 0, Phat(f+g) = Phat f, hence
  c_{R(f+g)}(n) - c_g(n) = c_{Rf}(n)   and   c_{f+g}(0) - c_g(0) = c_f(0),
which collapses the two cuspidal sums into the single sum above.
g-independence: two choices differ by an element of M_k, cancelling
in L*(f+g) - L*(g). QED.

REMARK (evaluation by opposite fixed points). Each piece is finite at
finite tau0, so basepoints are chosen independently: g in M_k^! has
H_g = H, is globally tau0-independent (no walls), read at tau0=i
(S-segment degenerates) as the section-4/BFK incomplete-gamma form;
f+g in F_k read at tau0->i*inf (T-segment dies, lem:cusp-decay(a)) as
the section-5/1806 polylog-projection form. The decomposition pairs
with the two fixed points that kill the two segments. No new integral
is computed -- the formula is a regrouping of eq:interior-pole-modes
and eq:bfk-eq-statement.
[NOTE: this corrects the looser claim made in discussion that the two
n!=0 sums "stay as two labelled pieces" -- they collapse to one sum
over c_{Rf}(n), because c_{R(f+g)} - c_g = c_{Rf}.]

REMARK (wall-crossing and scope). The interior wall-crossing of L*(f)
is inherited entirely from the f+g piece (g is wall-free), so L*(f) is
canonical up to the same interior wall-crossing as the regular-at-cusp
case (governed by the Proposition/Corollary of Addition A). The
hypothesis S_{2-k}=0 is what makes g exist: achievable cusp principal
parts are exactly those orthogonal to S_{2-k}. For negative weight
(1/Delta in F_{-12}, 1/E_6 in F_{-6}, where S_{2-k} != 0) the
obstruction is itself a residue identity: for G in S_{2-k},
  Res_cusp(f G dtau) = - sum_{tau_p} Res_{tau_p}(f G dtau)   on X(1),
generically nonzero, so f's cusp principal part need not be matchable
and the exact split fails -- fall back on the direct finite-contour
L*(f) (still finite by lem:Lstar-finite). Complete for this paper's
weights.
